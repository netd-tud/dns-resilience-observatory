Privacy

Privacy Policy

The Chair of Distributed and Networked Systems at TU Dresden processes personal data within the framework of the use of the publicly accessible website (dns-observatory.netd.cs.tu-dresden.de) only in the form of server logs. These include the IP addresses, Internet browser, operating system, accessed link and time of page access of visitors. This data is stored in the server logs for 60 days for information security reasons. This data will not be used for any other purpose. This data is only transmitted to third parties if this is stipulated by law. No tracking cookies are used to record or analyze user movements or the browsing behavior of users of our site.

Legal basis

The legal basis for this processing is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Rights of data subjects

Right to information (Art. 15 GDPR)

You have the right to request information about the data processed about you and the possible recipients of this data at any time. You are entitled to a reply within a period of one month after receipt of the request for information.

Right to rectification, erasure and restriction (Art. 16–18 GDPR)

You may at any time request TU Dresden to correct or erase your personal data or to restrict processing.

Voluntariness and revocation (Art. 7(3) GDPR)

Consent can be refused or revoked at any time informally and without giving reasons to the person responsible.

Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)

You may request that the controller transmit your personal data to you in machine-readable form. Alternatively, you may request the direct transfer of the personal data you have provided to another controller, where this is possible.

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)

You have the right to object, on grounds relating to your particular situation, at any time to processing of personal data concerning you which is based on Article 6(1)(e) GDPR. The controller will no longer process the personal data unless the controller demonstrates compelling legitimate grounds for the processing which override the interests, rights and freedoms of the data subject or for the establishment, exercise or defense of legal claims.

Right of appeal (Art. 77 GDPR)

You can contact the TU Dresden data protection officer at any time, as well as a supervisory authority for data protection in the event of a complaint pursuant to Art. 77 GDPR. The competent supervisory authority for TU Dresden is:

Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

This accessibility statement applies to https://dns-observatory.netd.cs.tu-dresden.de. This website is run by the Chair of Distributed and Networked Systems at TU Dresden (NETD). NETD is committed to making its websites accessible in accordance with the Website Accessibility Act, BfWebG (Barrierefreie-Websites-Gesetz), and the Accessible IT Ordinance, BITV 2.0 (Barrierefreie-Informationstechnik-Verordnung), implementing EU Directive 2016/2102.

Preparation of this accessibility statement

This statement was prepared on September 9, 2026. The basis for issuing this accessibility statement is self-assessment of the website’s compatibility with the requirements of the BfWebG in accordance with BITV 2.0.

Compliance status

No BITV self-assessment has been carried out to date.
